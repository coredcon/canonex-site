title: NPC memory, facts at the table, backups
date: 2026-10-02
version: Development build
summary: NPCs remember how the party treated them, Run shows what can be learned here, and every campaign backs itself up.
---

- **NPC memory**: when an NPC's attitude or a story tie changes, Canonex remembers it by itself, with the session and the scene. Adding a reason is optional.
- **Facts at the table**: Run lists what the party can learn in the current scene, so revealing a fact takes one click. **What the party knows** and **Recently learned** sit on Home, and every fact can answer **Who would know this?**
- **Your own relationships**: name a new kind of tie, with its wording from both sides, and change any tie in place without losing its notes.
- **The Graph** folds a party, group or faction into one disc, and secret ties stay on it, drawn dashed.
- **Backups**: a daily automatic backup of each campaign you open, plus one-click backups to a single `.canonex` file you can restore as a copy.
- **Characters from Wanderer's Guide**, beside Pathbuilder 2e and Foundry exports.
- **Foundry live sync** is now something you switch on, so the relay isn't polled while you prepare.
