---
title: Facts, and making something new
date: 2026-09-30
version: Development build
summary: Who knows what, as records you can tie and trace; and a Create window for original NPCs, creatures, items and places.
---

- **Facts**: a fact is a record of its own. It can be true, false, disputed or undecided. Tie the people who know it, suspect it or wrongly believe it, and Canonex keeps when they learned it.
- **Groups**: any record type can be a group, with members who join, leave and move between groups.
- **Icons**: choose from more than 5,000 icons for your own record types.
- **+ Create** on the GM bar makes **original** NPCs, creatures, items and places that are rules-correct for their level.
- **Bestiary** and **Equipment** browsers on the GM bar, with item pictures, and a **Loot** generator that rolls treasure by level.
