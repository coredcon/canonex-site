---
title: Campaign memory, themes and a better builder
date: 2026-09-25
version: Development build
summary: Canonex now remembers what changed, looks the way you want, and builds sessions and encounters by drag and drop.
---

- **Campaign memory** on Home: what changed since last session, open and quiet threads, returning faces, and forgotten prep.
- **Last time we saw…** on every record sheet, and filters for records never used in a session.
- **Trace a connection** in the Campaign Graph to light up the chain between two records.
- **Themes**: seven presets or your own colours, fonts, corners and background art.
- **Session builder**: the whole Codex beside the outline, ready to drag onto beats.
- **Encounter builder**: drag creatures, NPCs, Bestiary entries, places, factions and treasure onto an encounter. Every creature is its own participant, with Elite and Weak by the rules.
- **Reference cards** open web pages and PDFs inside the card.
- The Campaign Library shows each campaign's art, what's up next and the party.
- Deleting a campaign sends it to the Recycle Bin.
