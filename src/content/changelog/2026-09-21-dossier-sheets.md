date: 2026-09-21

- **Dossier sheets**: an NPC reads like a dossier, a creature like a combat instrument, with Location, Faction and player character sheets to match.
- **Native PF2e stat blocks** that match Foundry imports.
- **Foundry VTT bridge** through the REST API relay: link records to actors and pull their sheets.
- **Journals** on every record, **assets**, and a **Party** board.
- A **zoomable desk** with a minimap.
- Drag records onto sheet sections to tie them.
