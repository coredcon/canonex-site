summary: Capture a note from anywhere, and keep the tools you reach for mid-session in one bar.
---

- **Jot**: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd> anywhere to capture a note. Tag records with `@name`, or drop a record onto the note. During a live session it files into the session for wrap-up; while you prepare, it lands in the plan.
- **GM bar**: docks along the bottom during a session, with Jot, quick NPC, names by ancestry, DCs by level, clocks, rules lookup and **Start encounter**.
- Design work has begun on **Facts**, the foundation for tracking who knows what. See the [Roadmap](/roadmap/).
