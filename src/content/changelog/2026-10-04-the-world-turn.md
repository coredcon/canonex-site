---
title: The world turn
date: 2026-10-04
version: Development build
summary: The world keeps moving between sessions, written by the AI chat of your choice from what your campaign recorded.
---

- **The world turn**: say how much time passes and who acts. Canonex writes a briefing from the campaign: what the party did, each faction's and NPC's goals, where everyone is, and what they know. Paste it into any AI chat, paste the answer back, check it, and Canonex files it. Events go into each actor's memory, rumours become facts nobody knows yet, and hooks go into the next session's plan. It's all copy and paste: no account, no API key.
- **Goals**: NPCs and factions get long- and short-term goals, in a Goals card on their sheet.
- **Side cards** on every sheet fold from their heading, drag up and down, and can be shown or hidden per record type.
