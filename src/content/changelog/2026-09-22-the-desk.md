- **Pin** cards to the edge of the view and **lock** them in place; **snap to grid**.
- **Saved layouts** for scenes and runs, switched in an instant.
- **Reference tiles** for web addresses: paste a link onto the desk.
- **Undo and redo**, and **avoid overlaps**.
- Every record type has its own colour.
