title: Closed beta
date: 2026-10-08
version: 0.9 (closed beta)
summary: Canonex 0.9 goes to its first testers, with a Windows installer and a guided tour.
---

- **A Windows installer**: Canonex installs for your account only, with no admin rights needed. It adds a Start menu entry and an optional desktop shortcut. Updating installs over the old version, and uninstalling never touches your campaigns.
- **A guided tour** of the sample campaign, *Shadows over Katapesh*: the Codex, tying records by drag and drop, the Campaign Graph (where you tie two records yourself), Summon, the Desk, Sessions, Run, Encounters and the GM bar.
- **A new app icon**.
- The public beta follows on the [Download](/download/) page.
