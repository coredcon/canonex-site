---
title: Two game systems, ready out of the box
date: 2026-10-05
version: Development build
summary: Pathfinder 2e and fifth edition (5E) come with their open rules content, and a campaign speaks its system everywhere.
---

- **One system per campaign**: Pathfinder 2e, fifth edition (5E, 2024 rules) or your own. Sheets, DCs, encounter maths, the creature builder, treasure, names and the rules lookup all follow it.
- **5E included**: the SRD 5.2's 333 monsters, 340 spells and 633 items. Sheets use the 2024 stat block, Create builds creatures by challenge rating, encounters use the XP budget, and loot rolls by rarity.
- **Pathfinder 2e included**: the ORC-licensed remaster creatures, spells and equipment (1,193 creatures, 1,461 spells, 3,941 items), so the Bestiary is full before you import anything.
- **Bring what you own**: import 5E characters and creatures from Foundry exports, or the 5E content in your own Foundry install, including modules you bought. Nothing leaves your computer.
- **Groundwork: stat blocks from text and PDFs.** Canonex can now read stat blocks for both systems out of pasted text, and out of PDFs it opens itself. The window to use this comes next.
