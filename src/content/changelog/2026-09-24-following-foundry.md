---
title: Following Foundry, the Bestiary and sheet imports
date: 2026-09-24
version: Development build
summary: Live combat state from Foundry, a Bestiary from your own compendiums, and character imports from files.
---

- **Following Foundry**: combats started in Foundry appear in Canonex automatically, with live hit points, AC and conditions for every combatant. A Foundry combat can be recorded as a Canonex encounter.
- **The Bestiary**: creatures and NPCs by game system, built from the Foundry compendiums on your disk, with token art where available.
- **Import sheets** from Pathbuilder 2e JSON and Foundry actor exports.
- **Re-classify records** by dropping them on a type header in the Codex.
- Party members are tied to each other, and Canonex reopens where you left it.
- Areas open noticeably faster.
