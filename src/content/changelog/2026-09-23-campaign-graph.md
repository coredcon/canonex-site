---
title: The Campaign Graph, player view and custom types
date: 2026-09-23
version: Development build
summary: See the whole campaign as a web, share only what the players should see, and define your own record types.
---

- **The Campaign Graph**: every record and tie, with structural, story and secret ties drawn differently, and a Matrix view.
- **Player view**: a separate window that shows only the portrait, handout, map or read-aloud you choose, never the GM desk.
- **Custom record types**: a type editor, with type colours in Settings.
- **Layout prefabs**: save a proven desk arrangement and reuse it in any session.
- **Every record on the same sheet**, with shared templates for what many records have in common.
- The first version of **Canonex follows Foundry**.
