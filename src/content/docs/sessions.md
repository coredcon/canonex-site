## Plan

Create a session in **Sessions**. Give it a number, a title, the party level and, if you use milestones, the milestone it is building towards.

The **session outline** is made of **beats**: the scenes or moments you expect to reach. Open the **Builder** to see the whole Codex beside the outline, then drag in what each beat needs:

- records: NPCs, locations, factions, creatures
- encounters, or placeholders to build later
- maps and images
- read-aloud text
- notes

Anything that doesn't belong to a particular beat goes into **Loose material**. Each item can hold notes for this session only, such as *"begins hidden behind the machinery"*, without changing the record itself.

## Run

Use **Start Session** when the players sit down. The **Run** area becomes the cockpit for the session, and the **GM bar** docks along the bottom of the window:

- **Jot** — press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd> anywhere to capture a note. Type `@name` to tag a record.
- **NPC** — make an NPC on the spot and note that the party met them.
- **Names** — made-up names by ancestry, ready to copy or turn into an NPC.
- **DCs** — DCs by level around your party's level, with the difficulty adjustments.
- **Clock** — put a progress clock on the desk.
- **Rules** — search the rules in a reference card.
- **Start encounter** — the session's planned encounters come first.

As you play, mark outline items **reached**, **deferred** or **skipped**.

## Remember

At the end of the night, **wrap up** the session. Review the notes you jotted during play and promote the ones that matter into campaign canon. Material you didn't reach can **carry forward** into the next session, so no prep goes to waste.

Everything you changed is recorded, and the next time you open **Home**, campaign memory picks up where you left off.
