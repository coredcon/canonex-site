> 5E support is new in the 0.9 beta and still being refined.

Choose **fifth edition (5E)** when you create a campaign, and every tool speaks it. The 2024 rules come first; the 2014 rules are on the [roadmap](/roadmap/).

## What's included

Canonex comes with the **SRD 5.2**: 333 monsters, 340 spells and 633 items. The **Bestiary** and **Equipment** browsers on the GM bar are full from the first launch, with nothing to download or install.

## Sheets

- Creatures and NPCs use the **2024 stat block**. An **At the table** summary sits at the top: defences, saves, immunities and attacks.
- Player characters get a 5E character sheet, filled in by hand or imported.
- Record fields use 5E's words: **Species**, **Type**, **Challenge**.

## Encounters

Drag creatures onto an encounter and Canonex rates it against your party from the **XP budget**: low, moderate or high. Combatants bring their initiative.

## Making something new

- **+ Create** builds original NPCs and creatures by **challenge rating**, using benchmarks taken from the SRD's own creatures. Spellcasters get real spells from their class list.
- **Loot** rolls magic items by rarity for the party's level.

## Bringing in what you own

- **Foundry exports**: a 5E actor's **Export Data** file imports as a player character, NPC or creature.
- **Your Foundry install**: the Bestiary and the rules library can read the 5E packs in your own Foundry data folder, including modules for books you bought. They keep your art, and nothing leaves your computer.

Pasting a stat block or reading one from a PDF is coming next. The readers are already built, and the window that uses them follows.

## Words and rules

- The GM bar's **DCs**, **Names** and **Rules** lookup follow the campaign's system.
- NPC memory uses 5E's three attitudes.
