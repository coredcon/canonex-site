Canonex's core is system-neutral, and Pathfinder Second Edition is the first game system it supports in depth.

## Stat blocks

Creatures, NPCs and player characters get native PF2e stat blocks, laid out to match what you would see in a Foundry import. A creature's combat summary sits above the fold: level, hit points, AC, perception and speed.

## Importing characters

Import player characters from:

- **Pathbuilder 2e** JSON exports
- **Foundry VTT** actor exports, with totals calculated from the build

## The Bestiary

The **Bestiary** lists creatures and NPCs by game system. Canonex builds it from the Foundry compendiums already on your disk, including art from your token packs where Foundry maps it, so there is nothing extra to download.

## Encounters

- Drag creatures, NPCs, Bestiary entries, places, factions and treasure onto an encounter.
- Canonex rates the threat for your party, from **trivial** through **low**, **moderate** and **severe** to **extreme**.
- Every creature is its own participant, and **Elite** and **Weak** adjustments follow the rules.

## At the table

The GM bar's **DCs** list shows the DCs by level around your party's level, with the difficulty adjustments and simple DCs.
