Choose **Pathfinder 2e** when you create a campaign, and every tool speaks it: sheets, DCs, encounter threat, the creature builder, treasure and the rules lookup. See also [Fifth edition (5E)](/docs/fifth-edition/).

## Stat blocks

Creatures, NPCs and player characters get native PF2e stat blocks, laid out to match what you would see in a Foundry import. A creature's combat summary sits above the fold: level, hit points, AC, perception and speed.

## Importing characters

Import player characters from:

- **Pathbuilder 2e** JSON exports
- **Wanderer's Guide** exports
- **Foundry VTT** actor exports, with totals calculated from the build

## The Bestiary and Equipment

Canonex comes with the ORC-licensed remaster content: 1,193 creatures and NPCs, 1,461 spells and 3,941 items. The **Bestiary** and **Equipment** browsers on the GM bar are full from the first launch.

If you use Foundry VTT, Canonex can also add the compendiums already on your disk, including art from your token packs where Foundry maps it. A creature you import that way replaces the included copy.

## Encounters

- Drag creatures, NPCs, Bestiary entries, places, factions and treasure onto an encounter.
- Canonex rates the threat for your party, from **trivial** through **low**, **moderate** and **severe** to **extreme**.
- Every creature is its own participant, and **Elite** and **Weak** adjustments follow the rules.

## Making something new

**+ Create** on the GM bar makes original NPCs, creatures, items and places that fit the level you ask for. **Loot** rolls a party's treasure by level, and you choose how many uncommon, rare or unique items it may include.

## At the table

The GM bar's **DCs** list shows the DCs by level around your party's level, with the difficulty adjustments and simple DCs.
