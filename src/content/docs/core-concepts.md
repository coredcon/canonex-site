Canonex keeps a single model of your campaign, and each area shows that same model from a different angle. You enter something once and it is available everywhere.

## Records

A **record** is anything in your campaign that is worth remembering: an NPC, a creature, a location, a faction, a shop, a deity, a piece of lore, a clue, a quest, a journal or a player character.

Each record type has a **sheet** shaped for how you use it. An NPC reads like a dossier, a creature like a combat instrument, and a journal like a page. Every sheet keeps **secrets** and **GM notes** clearly apart from what the players might learn.

You can add **your own record types** in Settings, with their own fields and colour.

## Ties

A **tie** connects two records: *Rashida trusts Usilket*, *Harun fears Captain Vashti Moor*, *Lady Isolde funds Brother Ephraim*. A tie has a type and a direction, and it has a visibility:

- **Structural** ties describe how the world is arranged: member of, located in.
- **Story** ties are the ones that drive play: trusts, owes, fears.
- **Secret** ties are known only to the GM. They are drawn as dashed ember lines.

To tie two records, drag one record onto the other's sheet. The **Campaign Graph** shows every tie at once and can trace the chain between any two records.

## The desk

The **desk** is a zoomable workspace. Records become cards that you summon, move, resize, pin and dismiss. When you place two tied records near each other, the tie appears between them. See [The desk](/docs/the-desk/).

## Sessions

A **session** has an outline of beats, the material you plan to use, the party you expect, and after play, what actually happened. See [Plan, run, remember](/docs/sessions/).

## Campaign memory

Canonex keeps a change log of every record and tie. **Home** turns it into campaign memory: what changed since last session, the threads you left open, the faces about to return, and the prep you never used. Every sheet also shows **Last time we saw…**
